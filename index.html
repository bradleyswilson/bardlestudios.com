About the game

Play as Remy, a robot exploring the ruins of a civilization that lived around a magnetic pole. With a looming magnetic reversal, the builders split themselves into sentinels in an attempt to survive.

Push, pull, and pass charge through puzzles that intertwine with the world around them. Explore a story rich with secrets and wake what the builders left behind.
