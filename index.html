Declination

In development

Long ago, a civilization saw the planet's magnetic field about to flip. To survive, it split itself into pieces and left them behind as sentinels. Those sentinels have been asleep ever since.

You are a small robot whose job is to wake them. You explore a quiet, overgrown world and solve compact push-and-pull puzzles on locked grids. Each solve teaches you a rule, and every rule you learn changes what the rest of the world means.
