Some version of Declination has been floating around in my head for a couple of years now. It's time I bring it out into the world.

Declination is the story of an ancient civilization anchored to a magnetic pole and its collapse amidst a geomagnetic reversal. It's a story about how information is preserved and passed on among generations. It's a story inspired by absurdist philosophy and how to respond in an indifferent world. I believe deeply that the story of Declination is worth telling. It's my hope that it resonates with others.

## How it plays
Declination lives somewhere on the spectrum between a puzzle-adventure game and a metroidbrainia. You play as a robot named Remy exploring the world left behind by a civilization that split itself apart before the planet's magnetic field reversed.

It's built around small, tactical rearrangement puzzles that lean heavily into electromagnetism: push, pull, and transfer charge between objects to power ancient machinery. These puzzles intertwine with each other and the broader world to tell a non-linear, knowledge-heavy story inspired by games like Outer Wilds, Tunic, and Subnautica.
